Maintenance image credits

The maintenance overview uses the images from the corresponding Chinese Wikipedia templates. These local files are unchanged Wikimedia Commons originals. Each image retains its own license; common template images share one local file.

Templates without an explicit image inherit their actual message-box artwork from Module:Message box/configuration: content uses Ambox important.svg and style uses Edit-clear.svg. English and Japanese expansion templates share the image from Template:Expand language.

The style category combines several maintenance templates; its representative image comes from the contributing Overly detailed template. Citation needed, Verify source, and Dead link are text-only inline templates, so their cards have no icon. Unknown configured tasks also render without an icon.

The two deletion images use LGPL version 2.1 or later. Their source artwork is available from Nuvola filesystems trashcan full.png, Fairytale ark.png, Icono aviso borrar.png, and Delete.png. Other interface artwork has separate credits and Codex and Vue license notices.
