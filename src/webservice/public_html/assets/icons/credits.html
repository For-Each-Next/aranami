Review icon credits

These content-review icons follow Chinese Wikipedia Module:PJ:VG/RVW, revision 92100885. The candidate icons and community peer-review SVG are unchanged Wikimedia Commons files with local filenames. The project peer-review SVG is a native vector redraw of its Commons PNG source. Each image retains its own license.

The Codex history icon used for reassessment requests is by Wikimedia Design, the OOUI team, and other contributors, under CC BY 4.0. The local glyph comes from Codex revision dcd478e318d99a0361723557d7ed135ffb57a682, grouped into a currentColor symbol without further shape changes.

Complete GNU license texts: LGPL version 2.1 and GPL version 2. The affected icons permit the stated version or any later version. The interface stylesheet has a separate Codex license notice.

Main-page highlight icons represent the saved featured, good, or did-you-know appearance independently of the article's current assessment. These three SVG files are unchanged Commons originals.

Popularity medals

The six numbered medal SVGs are original Aranami artwork released under CC0 1.0. Their circular design follows the idea of Andrwsc's public-domain gold, silver, and bronze medal icons. The local artwork uses Codex presentation colors and original vector numerals; ranks 4–6 share a white background.
