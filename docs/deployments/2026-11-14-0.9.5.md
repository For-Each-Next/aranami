# Version 0.9.5 native editor checks and talk-header ordering

Version 0.9.5 accepts MediaWiki's section-zero trailing-whitespace
normalization, avoiding a false changed-page warning for an unchanged talk
revision. Revision, timestamp, page identity, title, destination and modified
draft checks still apply. The userscript fills the source and summary, opens
the native diff, and leaves saving to the editor.

Correction links and the read-only API use the checked talk page's decimal
revision ID. Internal source/proposal digests remain intact so existing saved
plans retain their integrity and availability. Manual repair summaries read
`Update talk-page article history.` without the Aranami attribution suffix.

Recognized top-level notices follow the verified Chinese
[talk-page layout guideline](https://zh.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=95581636).
Original DC8/talk through DC24/talk notices follow Article history and precede
project banners. Their parameters, flags and independent category behavior
remain intact. Unknown templates, comments, prose and shell contents retain
their source; boundary cleanup preserves indentation and trailing spaces on
neighboring nonblank lines. Older saved plans retain their insertion-boundary
fallback. The consistency source identity advances so normal startup collection
prepares fresh ranked plans.
