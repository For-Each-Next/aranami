# Version 0.9.4 source review and shared homepage data

Version 0.9.4 fills complete prepared talk-page leads and opens Wikipedia's
native source diff. The Tampermonkey script retains its fixed Toolforge job
lookup, explicit MediaWiki binding, title/revision/source guards and manual
publication step. Article history uses aligned block parameters without empty
lines around the template. Startup reprojects matching saved plans under the
existing output lease, keeping complete proposals and their source timestamps.

## Historical B-Class inference

An explicit Video games project class takes precedence. Missing or blank project
classes inherit a literal banner-shell class. If neither supplies a class, six
literal b1–b6 values of yes or y establish B, accepting case and whitespace.
Otherwise a missing grade is unassessed; hidden text, conflicting classes and
unsupported dynamic content remain unknown. The known historical Bckeck name
suffix is recognized without accepting arbitrary dynamic template names.

The previous 368/420 coverage had 52 pending articles: 49 stopped at an unknown
historical interval, two had a talk cutoff but no article cutoff, and one had not
formed a history decision. Rechecking the 49 exact blocking revisions classified
45 as unassessed and two as B; two remained unknown (hidden text and duplicate
project banners). These facts do not themselves complete article authorship:
new B intervals still require their exact article revision and WikiWho evidence.

The parser contract advances from 2 to 3. Completed proofs migrate only when
project aliases, shell aliases and the assessment profile agree exactly with the
audited compatible contract. Unknown scans reopen. Existing article evidence
survives unchanged nonnull page, talk, grade and listing boundaries; changed or
missing boundaries require a new cutoff without deleting older evidence.

## Newsletter homepage reuse

Newsletter homepage preparation consumes shared durable appearance evidence,
archive coverage, retained anniversary subjects and saved homepage records. It
performs no independent homepage wiki/API requests. Missing coverage remains
unknown; known partial results retain their incomplete marker.

Current open previews read the homepage's saved next-two-day estimates and mark
them as expected appearances. Forecasts remain separate from actual homepage,
DYK and statistical facts. Dated archives and published/cancelled issues keep
their saved observations. Read-only request tests forbid source clients, page
construction and directory writes.
