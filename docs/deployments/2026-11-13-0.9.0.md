## B attribution repair

The earlier 2/420 attribution result was primarily a source interpretation bug.
The cache contained 256 histories stopping as unknown at their selected first
revision, while 160 had not been attempted. The sampled pages placed their
explicit B/BL class on `WikiProject banner shell`; the nested Video games
banner omitted its own class. The previous extractor only read the child.

The corrected extractor follows the audited header-shell inheritance and
explicit child precedence contracts. Real retained heads for Timelie,
7 Days to End with You and the Fire Emblem game list reparse as B, B and BL.
Dynamic or ambiguous evidence remains unknown rather than manufacturing an
entry date. A new parser identity reconsiders the old records; unchanged
unsupported heads remain cached afterward.

The independent hourly idle author worker admits up to 80 history attempts
and 40 attribution attempts in a normal 240-second pass. History is capped
initially at 90 seconds; attribution receives the actual remaining time.
An isolated child, a nonblocking source lease, foreground priority and a
360-second hard wait bound memory and lifetime. Existing revision evidence
and exact article cutoffs remain reusable in Parquet.
