# Newsletter drafts

Use [Newsletter drafts](https://aranami.toolforge.org/newsletter/) to review
saved project facts and copy Chinese wikitext for manual publication. Opening
the page does not contact Wikipedia, fetch traffic, update files or save edits.
Background collection supplies the facts; editors supply the final review and
editorial prose.

## Choose an issue and date

Tools → Newsletter drafts opens the next scheduled unpublished issue. The menu
also offers earlier delayed drafts. Published/cancelled issues disappear from
the active menu but remain accessible through retained archive links. Selecting
an issue or date updates results in place; native GET submission works without
JavaScript. Stale responses cannot replace a newer selection.

Issue `2027-01` covers October–December 2026 in its ordinary pages and all of
2026 in its separate annual feature. Reporting intervals stay fixed even when
publication is delayed. Drafts refresh daily until publication/cancellation is
observed in the saved issue registry. Older dated archives remain immutable.

A data date means the inclusive completed day, normally available after the
following midnight capture. Its actual capture time appears separately.
Missing dates cannot be reconstructed by selecting them. Annual and quarterly
date streams have separate availability. Historical-source dependencies
belong to the [retirement checklist](../transition.md).

## Review and copy

Each accordion is labelled by its destination wiki page. Read the source in
its readonly expanding text area, use Copy, then follow View page or Edit page.
If Clipboard is unavailable, Copy selects the source for manual copying. The
edit link supplies a source-linked summary using the selected data date; it
does not publish the draft. Text remains literal wikitext.

Main, quality and announcement pages contain supported facts. Ordinary feature
and interview sections are blank editorial scaffolds; January instead has a
combined annual feature. Replace example images/captions and add editorial
prose before publishing. Only the two editor-comment invitations use the
verified reflection preload buttons.

Cutoff notices distinguish partial periods. Unknown figures use explicit
placeholder sentences or unavailable charts, not invented zeroes. Current
register corrections can improve a delayed draft while older selected dates
retain their original facts. Current formatting may improve when rendering an
old archive, without modifying its observations.

## Interpreting the annual feature

The four data sections remain `內容評審`, `新章推薦`, `条目报告` and
`榮譽表彰`, in that order. Quality lists follow actual recorded promotions and
removals, with source-linked dates and hidden MMDD editorial markers. Each
article counts once at its surviving grade for the year; later demotions do
not rewrite older cohorts. Nomination credits remain distinct from listing-time
text authorship. Explicit non-main-author claims remain visible; unknown roles
are not guessed.

DYK statistics count first appearances rather than repeats. Supported game-year
categories precede observed fictional-character, biography/company and music
memberships; unknown classifications remain `待分類`. Annual charts extend
only evidenced historical series. Article stock changes are not automatically
newly created articles, and snapshot revision-ID differences are not edit counts.

Pageviews uses calendar-year observations, not a rolling year. Detailed measured
daily rows support annual totals and rankings; incomplete coverage keeps totals
unknown while useful measured entries may remain. Titles, assessments and
task-force membership come from the midnight project dataset even when traffic
arrives later. Monthly winners retain exact source periods and revision links
when available. Earlier saved date selections do not change when traffic or
register evidence later improves.

The new-article summary updates each month's count from the curated register
through the selected completed day. For example, November registrations update
`11月計…篇` in the October–December issue throughout November.

Annual ACG awards include all positive gains and mark first-year scorers with
`N`. Quarterly awards retain their own top-five/tie and newcomer rules.
Community honors and prose remain editorial work. A current snapshot cannot
recreate a missing historical endpoint. The
[retirement checklist](../transition.md) tracks migration dependencies.

[Collection contracts](../tasks/newsletter.md) explain cutoffs, storage and
migration. [Saved data](../data/storage.md) defines the independent
project, traffic and event datasets behind the interface.

Current draft previews reuse the homepage's saved next-two-day estimates and
mark them as expected appearances. They make no independent wiki/API requests
and never add forecasts to proven appearance counts. Frozen and dated issue
views retain their own saved observations.
